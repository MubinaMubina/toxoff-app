1. What toxoff does

toxoff connects to Instagram professional accounts you own or manage and removes comments on your posts that match your filters, using automated checks. By default, comments the checks are highly confident are abusive are deleted from Instagram permanently, and the rest are hidden so you can review and restore them in the app. In Filters you can choose to hide everything, or to delete everything. Deleted comments cannot be restored.

2. Automated checks can be wrong

toxoff uses automated tools, including AI, to decide which comments to remove. They can miss harmful comments and can remove comments that were fine. Review your removed comments and restore hidden ones that should stay. Deletions cannot be undone. toxoff does not guarantee that every harmful comment will be caught.
