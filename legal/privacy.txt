TOXOFF PRIVACY POLICY

Effective: 14 September 2026

toxoff ("toxoff", "we", "us") is an iPhone app that removes toxic and spam comments on your
Instagram posts. This policy explains what information toxoff collects, why, who it is shared
with, and how you can delete it.

Contact: support@toxoff.app


1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Your toxoff account
- Your name and email address, from sign-up or from Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple.
  (Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one.)
- Your password, if you sign up with email. It is stored only in hashed form by our sign-in
  provider; we never see it.

Your connected Instagram account
- Your Instagram account ID and username.
- An access token from Instagram that lets toxoff read, hide and delete comments on your posts.
  It is kept on our servers and is never shown in the app.

Comments on your posts
- toxoff reads new comments on your recent Instagram posts so it can check them.
- For each comment it checks, we keep only the comment's ID, for up to 7 days, so the same
  comment is never checked twice.
- For comments toxoff hides (or deletes, if you choose that in Filters), we keep the comment
  text, the commenter's username, why it was removed, and which post it was on, so you can
  review it in the app and restore hidden ones.
- Comments that are not removed are not stored.

Your settings
- Your filter settings: sensitivity, categories, blocked keywords and blocked usernames.
- Whether you want push notifications, and your device's push notification token.

Your plan and usage
- Your plan (Free, Solo or Plus), trial dates, how many free comment checks you have used, and
  any extra checks earned from invites.
- Your invite codes and whether a friend used them.
- If you subscribe: your plan, billing period, renewal date and subscription status, as reported
  by Apple through RevenueCat. Payments are handled by Apple. We never receive your card or
  other payment details.

Technical data
- Short-lived counters used to limit how many comments are checked per minute (deleted within
  an hour), and technical error logs kept for a limited time to fix problems.


2. HOW WE USE IT

We use this information only to run toxoff:
- to check comments on your posts and hide (or, if you choose, delete) the ones that match
  your filters;
- to show you removed comments and let you restore hidden ones;
- to send you a notification when a comment is removed (notifications do not include the
  comment's text);
- to manage your trial, plan, free checks, invites and subscription;
- to keep the service secure and prevent abuse of free checks and invites.

We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use your information or
your commenters' comments for any purpose other than moderating your own posts.


3. WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

We use these service providers, only for the purposes above:
- Supabase (database, sign-in and servers, hosted in Singapore).
- OpenAI, whose AI model scores comment text for harassment, hate, slurs, spam and self-harm in
  any language. Only the comment text is sent. Under OpenAI's API policies, this data is not used
  to train OpenAI's models.
- Meta (Instagram), to read, hide and delete comments on your posts through Instagram's
  official API.
- Apple and RevenueCat, to process and verify subscriptions.
- Expo, to deliver push notifications.
- Google and Apple, if you choose to sign in with them.

We may also disclose information if required by law.


4. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

- Your account, settings and removed-comment history: until you delete your account.
- Comment IDs used to avoid double checks: up to 7 days.
- Your Instagram access token: until you disconnect the account in toxoff, remove toxoff in
  Instagram's settings, or delete your toxoff account.
- To prevent abuse of invites, we keep a one-way hash (a scrambled code that cannot be turned
  back into the ID) of each Instagram account ever connected, even after deletion. It is not
  linked to you once your account is deleted.


5. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

- Disconnect Instagram at any time in toxoff. toxoff stops reading your comments and deletes
  the access token.
- Remove toxoff from Instagram: Instagram > Settings > Apps and websites > toxoff > Remove.
- Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it.
- Delete your account and data: see https://sjfmcieunormozrybqmi.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/legal/delete-data.txt
  or email support@toxoff.app from your account's email address.


6. CHILDREN

toxoff is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country),
and we do not knowingly collect their information.


7. CHANGES

If we change this policy, we will post the new version at this address with a new effective
date, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.


8. CONTACT

Questions or requests: support@toxoff.app
